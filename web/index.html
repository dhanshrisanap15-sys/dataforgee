How it works

Three ideas that make it feel like a person, not a test.

01

Continuous dual-stream audio

Never hit a record button again. As you speak, our streaming acoustic model parses your voice frame by frame without freezing the dialogue.

Micro-phoneme heatmaps

See exactly which syllables drift. Mouth-shape guides and pitch contour overlays show you the fix instead of handing you a percentage.
